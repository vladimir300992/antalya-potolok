What affects the final price?

Area alone is not enough for a final quote. Perimeter length, corners, curtain niches, beams, sloped sections, spotlights, chandeliers, tracks, linear lighting, ventilation and AC details all affect labour and materials. For the first estimate, send the district, room list, dimensions, preferred ceiling type and lighting plan.

Where we work

We install stretch ceilings in Antalya and travel to Kemer, Belek, Side, Alanya and other locations by prior agreement. Travel conditions are confirmed during the preliminary estimate.
