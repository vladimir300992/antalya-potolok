Why area alone is not enough

Two rooms with the same floor area can have very different perimeter lengths. A 4 × 5 m room has 20 m² of area and 18 m of perimeter, while a 2 × 10 m room has the same 20 m² but 24 m of perimeter. That means more profile, more fastening and more installation work.

Corners, beams, curtain niches, spotlights, tracks, linear lighting, ventilation and service access also affect the final price. This is why multiplying area by a starting rate gives only a first reference point.

What to send for a preliminary estimate

Send the district, room list, dimensions of each room, preferred ceiling type and lighting requirements. If you have a plan, attach it in WhatsApp. Mention curtain niches, track length and any sloped or high ceiling sections.

Payment and measurement

Materials are paid with a 100% advance. Labour is split 50% at the start of installation and 50% after completion. Measurement in Antalya costs 1,000 TRY and is fully credited toward the order advance; if the advance is paid during the visit, measurement is free.
