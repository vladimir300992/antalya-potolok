TRACK + STRETCH CEILING

Track lighting in stretch ceilings in Antalya

An integrated track gives you movable, directional fixtures while keeping the ceiling geometry clean and coordinated.

Where track lighting is useful

Tracks work well in living rooms, kitchens, corridors, shops and offices where light needs to adapt to furniture, artwork or changing functional zones. Fixtures can be repositioned and redirected without rebuilding the ceiling.

How we integrate the system

The track is not simply attached after the ceiling is finished. Its position, support, electrical feed and relation to the stretch-ceiling profile are agreed in advance. This allows the track to sit cleanly in the ceiling plane and avoids improvised junctions.

Price

Projects with integrated track lighting are premium solutions starting from $60/m². Final cost depends on track type and length, number of fixtures, supports, ceiling configuration and other lighting elements.

What to decide before installation

  • track length and position;
  • furniture and functional zones;
  • number and type of track fixtures;
  • other light sources in the room;
  • curtain niches, ventilation and technical ceiling elements.

Preliminary estimate

Send a plan or room dimensions, approximate track length and number of fixtures. We will prepare the first calculation and identify what needs to be checked during measurement.
