ANTALYA STRETCH CEILING

Ceiling and lighting as one project

We install stretch ceilings in Antalya and coordinate the ceiling profile, lighting, curtain niches and technical details before installation begins.

What we focus on

Our work is not limited to stretching a white membrane. We handle standard ceilings, shadow-gap profiles, floating LED details, track systems, linear lighting, curtain niches and the technical interfaces around ventilation and air-conditioning.

For membrane projects we use BAUF solutions where specified and provide a two-year warranty on our ceiling work. The exact materials and configuration are fixed in the estimate before installation.

How we work

We begin with a preliminary calculation from dimensions or a plan. After the main scope is clear, we measure the site, confirm the final estimate and prepare materials. Installation is then coordinated as a single ceiling-and-lighting task rather than separate disconnected jobs.

Transparent pricing

Starting levels are published on the site: standard solutions from $30/m², shadow-gap solutions from $45/m², premium LED/track/linear-light projects from $60/m², and bathrooms from $50/m². The final price depends on perimeter, corners, niches, lighting and construction details.

Service area

We work in Antalya and travel to Kemer, Belek, Side, Alanya and other nearby areas by prior agreement. For projects outside Antalya, travel conditions are confirmed during the preliminary estimate.

Payment and measurement

Materials are paid with a 100% advance. Labour is paid 50% at installation start and 50% after completion. Measurement in Antalya costs 1,000 TRY and is fully credited toward the order advance; if the advance is paid during the visit, measurement is free.
