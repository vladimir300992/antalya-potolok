What to send for a preliminary estimate

Please include the district, which rooms need ceilings, approximate area or room dimensions, the preferred ceiling type and the type/quantity of lighting. If you do not know the technical names, simply send a reference photo and we will identify the closest solution.

Measurement conditions

Measurement in Antalya costs 1,000 TRY and is fully credited toward the order advance. If the advance is paid during the measurement visit, the visit is free. For locations outside Antalya, travel conditions are confirmed during the preliminary estimate.
