INTEGRATED LINEAR LIGHT

Linear lighting in stretch ceilings in Antalya

Integrated light profiles make illumination part of the ceiling design and help define room geometry and functional zones.

Light as part of the ceiling

Linear lighting can work as a visual feature, useful task light or both. It can be combined with spotlights, chandeliers, tracks and a shadow-gap perimeter. The line should be positioned relative to furniture, circulation routes and working zones rather than placed only for symmetry.

How we plan it

We agree the length and shape of each line, LED power, switching groups, colour temperature and access to drivers. Straight lines, L-shapes and more complex layouts are possible when the ceiling frame and lighting are coordinated in advance.

Price

Projects with integrated linear lighting are premium solutions starting from $60/m². Final cost depends on total line length, number of corners and intersections, LED specification, drivers, ceiling geometry and other lighting elements.

What we need for the first calculation

  • room plan or dimensions;
  • approximate furniture and working zones;
  • preferred length and geometry of the light lines;
  • other light sources such as spots, chandeliers or tracks;
  • curtain niches and technical ceiling details.

Why lighting should be agreed early

When light and ceiling are designed together, we can set the ceiling drop, niches, power points and service access before installation. This reduces late changes and makes the final geometry cleaner.
