QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Your questions, answered

What do you need for a preliminary estimate?

Send the district, rooms, area or dimensions, ceiling type, recess lengths and lighting details. A plan and photos are helpful.

What is included in the price per square metre?

The prices are starting levels. Perimeter, corners, recesses, lighting and installation complexity affect the total. The written quote specifies the exact materials and work.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on room sizes, ceiling details and lighting. We agree on the schedule after measurement and approval of the design.

Can you install a ceiling in a bathroom?

Yes. Bathroom ceilings start from $50/m². We plan ventilation, lighting and access to services before installation.

When should I contact you during renovation?

Ideally before electrical work and final decoration are finished, so we can plan height, recesses and lighting. If the renovation is complete, send photos to discuss the options.

Can I book a measurement visit?

A visit in Antalya costs 1,000 TRY, credited in full toward the order advance. If the advance is paid during the visit, measurement is free. Travel outside the city is agreed in advance.

Is a stretch ceiling suitable for Antalya’s climate?

We install stretch ceilings in Antalya, including humid rooms. Ventilation and operating conditions are considered for each space. Open balconies and extreme temperatures require a separate assessment.

Is BAUF suitable for a child’s room?

BAUF publishes DIN EN 71 testing and an A+ emissions rating. You can review the manufacturer’s documents; the selected membrane is specified in your quote.

Is a stretch ceiling non-combustible?

Fire performance depends on the specific membrane. Standard PVC should not be described as non-combustible. Tell us about any special fire requirements before the quote so the material can be selected against its certificate and the project requirements.

How long does it last, and what is the warranty?

Service life depends on the material, installation and use. Our ceilings have a 2-year warranty with terms set out in the contract. Protect the membrane from sharp objects and strong local heat.

What should I do after a leak from upstairs?

Do not puncture the membrane or drain it yourself. Do not use wet light fittings; contact us for an inspection. Whether it can be restored depends on water temperature and the condition of the membrane and fixings.

Can a ceiling sag?

Small deviations can depend on room size and air pressure. Noticeable sagging, inward movement or waves need inspection of the fixings, ventilation and ceiling void.

How do I clean the ceiling?

Use a soft cloth and a product suitable for the selected membrane. Avoid abrasives, stiff brushes and solvents. Do not press hard or touch it with sharp objects.

Should I move furniture? Will there be dust?

We need access to the walls and light positions. We discuss large furniture and protection before work begins. Profile installation involves drilling, so room preparation is agreed in advance.

Why do prices differ between countries?

Material purchasing and delivery, profiles, lighting and labour all affect the price. Compare like-for-like specifications; our quote lists the agreed project scope.

Why choose your team?

We plan ceilings and lighting together, prepare a preliminary estimate and agree on the full quote before installation. We use BAUF, discuss extra work in advance and provide a 2-year warranty.
