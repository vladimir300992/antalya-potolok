SHADOW-GAP PROFILE

Shadow-gap stretch ceiling in Antalya

A precise dark line between wall and ceiling replaces the decorative perimeter insert and gives the room a cleaner architectural finish.

How the shadow gap works

A dedicated aluminium profile holds the membrane away from the wall and creates a controlled open gap. The dark line emphasizes room geometry and works especially well with flush walls, curtain niches, track lighting and linear light.

Where it works best

Shadow-gap ceilings are commonly chosen for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and modern commercial spaces. The detail is visually minimal, so wall preparation matters more than with a standard profile: uneven plaster or irregular corners remain more visible.

Price

Starting level: from $45/m². Final cost depends on area and perimeter, number of corners, curtain niches, beams, lighting and special junctions. A narrow room with the same area as a square room can require more profile and labour because its perimeter is longer.

What we agree before installation

  • room dimensions and perimeter;
  • wall readiness and corner geometry;
  • curtain niches and furniture junctions;
  • spotlights, chandeliers, tracks or linear lighting;
  • ventilation, AC and service access.

Before the site visit

For a preliminary estimate, send the district, room sizes or plan, preferred ceiling type and lighting requirements. If you already know the approximate niche or track length, include it. This lets us prepare a realistic first estimate before measurement.
